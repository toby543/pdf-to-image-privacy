Privacy Policy

PDF to Image · Last updated: 9 October 2026

PDF to Image converts PDF files into images on your own device. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What the app does with your data

  • Your PDFs and the images made from them never leave your device. All conversion happens locally. The app does not upload files, and it has no account, sign-in or server.
  • No data is collected. The app does not collect, store, share or sell personal information, and it contains no advertising, analytics or tracking libraries.
  • Passwords you enter to open protected PDFs are used only in memory while converting. They are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings (format, quality, resolution, page range, chosen folder) are stored only on your device so the app can remember them next time.
  • App lock (optional) uses Android's own unlock screen: fingerprint, face, or your PIN, pattern or password. The app never sees, receives or stores your biometric data or your screen lock.
  • Security choices (app lock on or off, hide from screenshots, clear password) are stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Files you choose – the app reads only the PDFs you pick or share to it, and writes images only to the gallery (Pictures/PDF to Image) or to a folder you choose.
  • Notifications (optional) – to show conversion progress. The app works without this permission.
  • Foreground service (data sync) – keeps a conversion running if you switch to another app.
  • Biometric / fingerprint (optional) – only so the app lock can show Android's unlock screen. Nothing is read from or sent anywhere.

The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your files or any other data anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
